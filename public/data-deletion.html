1. About This Page

This page describes how users may request deletion of personal information associated with AUTOPAL ERP.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

Users should send a data deletion request by email to admin@autopal.com.

The request should include:

  • Full name
  • Registered mobile number
  • Registered email address
  • Company or organisation name, where applicable
  • A clear statement requesting deletion of AUTOPAL ERP data

3. Identity Verification

Additional information may be requested to verify identity and prevent unauthorised deletion requests.

4. Processing of Requests

After verification, eligible personal information will be deleted or anonymised.

5. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Tax and accounting records
  • Audit requirements
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • Contractual obligations
  • Legitimate business record retention

6. Completion Notification

The user may be informed when the request has been processed.

Users can review the Privacy Policy at /privacy.html.
