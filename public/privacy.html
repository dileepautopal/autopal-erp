1. Introduction

AUTOPAL ERP is operated by Autolite (India) Limited, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when users access or use the AUTOPAL ERP application.

2. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that is required to provide and manage AUTOPAL ERP services, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Company or business details
  • Customer, distributor, retailer, technician, employee, or business partner information
  • Login and account information
  • Product enquiry and order information
  • WhatsApp messages sent to the application
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser, device, and application logs

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing ERP and business services
  • Customer and partner communication
  • Processing enquiries and orders
  • Proforma invoice and transaction processing
  • User authentication
  • Customer support
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Improving application performance
  • Legal and regulatory compliance

4. WhatsApp and Meta Services

AUTOPAL ERP may use WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta services for authorised business communication. Information received through WhatsApp may be processed to respond to enquiries, create records, support customers, or process business transactions.

5. Sharing of Information

Information is not sold. It may be shared only with authorised employees, service providers, technology providers, regulators, auditors, or legal authorities when necessary.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational security measures are used to protect information. However, no internet system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for business, legal, accounting, audit, security, and regulatory purposes.

8. User Rights

Users may have the following rights, subject to applicable law and verification:
